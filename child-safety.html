Flank Tabletop — Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 18 March 2026

Flank Tabletop ("Flank") is operated by Ayirga Media. We are committed to creating a safe environment for all users and have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

Flank strictly prohibits any content, behaviour, or activity that sexually exploits or endangers children. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form
  • Grooming or solicitation of minors
  • Any sexualised content involving minors
  • Facilitating or encouraging any form of child exploitation

2. Age Requirements

Flank is not directed at children under 13. Users must be at least 13 years of age to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

3. Detection and Reporting

  • User-generated content (messages, profiles, photos) that violates this policy will be removed immediately upon discovery.
  • Accounts found to be in violation will be permanently banned without warning.
  • We will report confirmed or suspected CSAE material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via their CyberTipline, and to relevant law enforcement authorities.
  • We will cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

4. User Reporting

Users can report suspected CSAE or any safety concern by:

  • Emailing safety@ayirga.media
  • Using the in-app reporting features on profiles and messages

All reports are treated as urgent and will be reviewed promptly.

5. Accountability

Ayirga Media is responsible for enforcing this policy. Questions or concerns about our child safety practices can be directed to safety@ayirga.media.
