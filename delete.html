Flank Tabletop — Account & Data Deletion

We respect your right to delete your account and all associated data. You can request deletion using either method below.

Option 1: In-App

Open Flank Tabletop → Settings → Delete Account. This will immediately schedule your account and all data for permanent deletion.

Option 2: Email Request

Send an email to privacy@ayirga.media with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Include the email address associated with your Flank account so we can verify your identity.

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed within 30 days:

  • Your profile (display name, username, bio, avatar, location)
  • All armies and army photos
  • Battle results and statistics
  • Messages and conversations
  • Meet requests
  • Kudos ratings (given and received)
  • Badges and leaderboard entries
  • Push notification tokens
  • Premium subscription status (active subscriptions should be cancelled separately via the App Store or Google Play)

What Is Retained

Anonymised, aggregated statistics (e.g. total app-wide game counts) that cannot be linked back to you may be retained.
